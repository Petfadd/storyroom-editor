## Section 6 — standing editor instructions

Follow the storyboard exactly after visual QC; do not treat this candidate plan as permission to guess.

1. **One shot per cue, in order.** Use exactly the `Srt001`–`Srt120` blocks and their full source filenames. Do not add, skip, merge, split, reorder, repeat, substitute or invent shots.
2. **Exact cuts.** Use each listed source IN and exclusive OUT and its Narration timeline. Conform in strict mode: no automatic sliding, easing, trimming, extending, rounding, or shifting to land on a breath or avoid a scene change. Snap only to the nearest valid frame boundary and show any frame-level deviation per shot.
3. **Playback.** Play video at `1.0×`, looping OFF, source audio muted. Never extract a frame, screenshot, freeze, loop, slow, reverse, or add artificial motion.
4. **Zero tolerance.** Never show anchors, studio presenters/co-anchors, studios/newsrooms, reporters on camera (including standups/live shots), previews/teasers, intros/outros, bumpers, title/end cards, branded transitions, static inserts, unrelated footage, or a wrong identified person. If any appears in the shot or its checked margins, stop and report the affected Srt entry; do not fix it silently.
5. **Identity and context.** Follow each Visual direction and Continuity warning. Neutral case context is not original evidence: an exterior is not a kitchen; a riverbank is not a storm drain; visible movement does not reveal hidden contents or intent; a public interview is not bodycam, 911 audio, or a confession. Do not show the victim while the suspect is named, or the suspect while the victim is named.
6. **Fields and captions.** Continuity is an editing note and is never rendered. Render only the literal On-screen caption when it is not `None`. Do not change the SRT or add burned-in subtitles.
7. **Stop and report.** If a file is missing/mismatched, a trim is out of bounds, a duration or overlap is wrong, identity/context is uncertain, or any prohibited element is present, leave that shot unassigned and report the Srt number and reason. Before export, provide a per-shot comparison of script IN/OUT against what was placed.
8. **Rights and render.** The user must record the editor’s reuse decision for every selected asset before final render. This package does not clear rights.
